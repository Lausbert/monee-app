# Delete your Monee account

You can delete your account yourself by opening Monee on your device. Then tap on 'Settings' (gear icon) and then on 'Profile'. Now tap on 'Delete Profile'. All your data will be permanently deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

Alternatively, feel free to send me your Apple or Google Account to [info@monee-app.com](mailto:info@monee-app.com) and I'll take care of it for you.

I’m happy if you ever feel like using Monee again.
