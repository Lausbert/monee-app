### Why launch during the holidays?

It actually fits the Monee philosophy perfectly.

By the 28th, the holiday rush is over. The gifts are unwrapped (and paid for). It’s usually the moment when my wife and I sit down, look at our joint account, and take a deep breath. It’s the best time to reset and look at the year ahead.

That’s exactly what Monee is for.

I didn’t build this app to be another subscription you forget about. I built it because, with two small kids (5 and 2) and a busy job, I needed clarity. I don’t want "smart" algorithms guessing my categories. I want to know exactly what’s coming in and what’s going out.

We believe that **manually tracking expenses** is the only way to really stay conscious of your spending. It takes five seconds to enter a purchase, but that tiny friction point makes you think. It stops the mindless consumption that’s so easy to fall into—especially when buying things online late at night.
